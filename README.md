Two lines explaining one everyday thing — a queue — to a 10-year-old. Line 1 is a single vivid sentence built on one analogy; line 2 says in one sentence why that analogy works: what it maps and what it leaves out.

> A queue is a snake made of people that creeps forward one step every time its head gets served.
> It maps how one slow person at the front holds everybody back, but it leaves out that a real snake chooses where it goes, while nobody in a queue can step out without losing their place.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the two lines to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`). Deterministic, stdlib only, offline. The program asserts the sealed gate itself: UTF-8 text of 1–400 characters, exactly two non-empty lines.
